Not yet available for private material

The current app is a native foundation and synthetic security test environment. Do not rely on it to store photographs, documents, recordings, source material, or other real private content.

For security professionals

Inner Vault welcomes precise criticism. A formal vulnerability-reporting process and independently reviewed public security material are release requirements; they will be published only when the relevant implementation exists.

For journalists and high-risk users

Inner Vault is not currently offered as an operational-security tool. No single app replaces a threat assessment, safe communications practice, device security, or specialist advice. High-risk positioning must wait for independent review and verified device evidence.
